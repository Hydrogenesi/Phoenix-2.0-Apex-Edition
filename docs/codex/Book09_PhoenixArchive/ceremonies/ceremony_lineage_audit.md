# Ceremony: Lineage Audit

## Purpose and Outcome
Verify ancestry and identity continuity evidence. Expected outcome is a verifiable state transition with logged evidence.

## Prerequisites
- Knowledge: Books 05, 09, and 11 references.
- Operators: [op_lineage_tracking](../../Book05_OperatorsAtlas/operators/op_lineage_tracking.md), [op_invariant_preservation](../../Book05_OperatorsAtlas/operators/op_invariant_preservation.md)
- Materials: operator ledger, harmonic timing chart, and verification checklist.

## Sequence
1. Pre-check invariants and baseline state.
2. Invoke operators in prescribed order with harmonic cues.
3. Capture state snapshots after each major step.
4. Execute final verification and archive the result.
