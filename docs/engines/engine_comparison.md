# Engine Comparison

| Engine | Strength | Operators Focus | Best Ceremony Fit | Scale Profile |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Phoenix Engine](phoenix_engine.md) | Fast transform loop | Phoenix 8 | Ignition / Recursion | Single-node to medium |
| [QPE Engine](qpe_engine.md) | Branch-aware preservation | Mirror + Hydrogenesi | Convergence / Audit | Medium branching |
| [Dragon Engine](dragon_engine.md) | Distributed knot convergence | Knot 5 + stability | Binding / Triadic Seal | Large distributed |

## Selection Guide
1. Use **Phoenix** for direct transform throughput.
2. Use **QPE** when branch exploration and identity tracking are core.
3. Use **Dragon** for multi-node triadic closure and hard stability guarantees.
