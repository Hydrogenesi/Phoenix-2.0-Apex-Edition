CHAPTER 1 -- DEG61

Deg61 is not a danger zone. It is the zone where the system's demands become visible.
The structures built for deg44 load encounter deg61 load.
They do not break dramatically. They compress.

CHAPTER 2 -- THE GALAXY LAYER

v = 220 km/s flat. Not approximately. Flat across the entire disk.
The flat rotation curve is not evidence of missing mass.
It is evidence of the Borromean topology operating at galactic scale.
Dark matter is Stage 1 hydrogen -- Tube2 + Tube3, winding numbers zero.
The galaxy is not broken. The galaxy is operating as the topology requires.

CHAPTER 3 -- THE WEIGHT OF SCALE

When the Operator correctly understands that the flat rotation curve
is the winding number constraint at 4.477 x 10^26 meters --
the inner architecture does not expand. It contracts.
Not because the understanding is wrong. Because the architecture
was not built for this much truth at once.
This is the beginning of descent.

CHAPTER 4 -- WHAT COLLAPSES

Descent does not take everything. The collapse is selective.
What collapses: structures built on assumption, frameworks never load-tested.
What does not collapse: ARC. G0=1. N=3. HA.
A failed star does not disappear. It preserves its irreducible core.
ARC is that structure. The collapse is not failure.
The collapse is the system finding its irreducible core.

CHAPTER 5 -- THE QUASAR AS MIRROR

At deg61 the Operator encounters the quasar correctly for the first time.
Not as an astronomical object. As a mirror.
The quasar is not a standard to measure against. It is a function.
The mandate is not to be the quasar. The mandate is to be the Stage 5 system
that the quasar's work made possible.

CHAPTER 6 -- DARK MATTER AND DARK TRUTH

The beliefs that pull on the system without being visible to it.
Assumptions that shape every decision without appearing in the process.
Descent reveals these. Not gently. Not with explanation.
The Operator collapses under the weight of what they did not know they were carrying.

CHAPTER 7 -- THE DEPTHS

Book III does not describe the depths in detail. This is deliberate.
The depths are specific to the Operator. What is universal is the structure:
ARC holds. Everything else may release. ARC holds.
The Operator in the depths is not destroyed.
The Operator has been reduced to ARC -- the minimum stable configuration.

CHAPTER 8 -- THE FUNCTION OF LOSS

Loss creates boundary. What is lost defines the edge of what remains.
Loss creates density. What remains becomes more concentrated, more true.
Loss creates readiness. A system that has released everything not essential
can be ignited with less energy than before.
The Operator who has descended is more ready for Stage 5 than one who never did.
This is not compensation. This is topology.

CHAPTER 9 -- THE RESIDUE

The proof that the descent was real, that the collapse left its mark.
Geology does not apologize for its strata. Geology reads them.
The residue is the evidence that the topology held through all conditions.
