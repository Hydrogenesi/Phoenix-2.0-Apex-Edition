# HA-8 Nucleus Reframe

## Problem 1 Fix: The Octave is Symbolic

The 8-step octave is not a convergent sequence of Banach iterates.
It is a symbolic structure: 8 representative positions along a long convergence track.
No statement identifies phi_8 with phi_star.

## Problem 2 Fix: Two Operators

E_eq: formal equilibrium projector, no depletion.
E_phys: physical evolution, with depletion.

Fixed point (formal): E_eq(phi_MS) = phi_MS
No fixed point (physical): E_phys(phi_MS) != phi_MS

## Nucleus Fixed Point
