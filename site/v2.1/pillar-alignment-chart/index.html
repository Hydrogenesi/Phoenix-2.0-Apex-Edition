    Overview¶

    The v2.1 Pillar Alignment Chart maps how the three pillars—Phoenix (🔥), Hydrogenesi (🌊), and The Third (🔗)—express themselves across each of the eight engine phases. While each phase is primary to one pillar, all three pillars remain active and present throughout the entire cycle, maintaining their essential functions.

    This chart reveals: - Primary pillar attribution for each phase - Secondary and tertiary pillar roles in support - Cross-pillar harmonics and resonance patterns - Triadic symmetry preserved through the cycle - Convergence dynamics as all pillars unite at apex

    Triadic Pillar Essence¶

    Phoenix (🔥)¶

    Nature: Transformation and ignition
    Function: Pattern creation, destruction, and renewal
    Operators: ⊕ ⊗ ⊛ △ ⊝ ⊞ ⊳ ⊲
    Domain: Ascent Phase (primary), All phases (supportive)

    Hydrogenesi (🌊)¶

    Nature: Preservation and structural integrity
    Function: Identity tracking, lineage maintenance, continuity
    Operators: Identity anchoring, Lineage tracking, Continuity mapping, Recursion, Source tracing
    Domain: Flight Phase (primary), All phases (supportive)

    The Third (🔗)¶

    Nature: Binding and unification
    Function: Knot topology, governance, synthesis
    Operators: B (Base), C (Cross-pillar), T (Triadic), A (Apex), S (Seal)
    Domain: Return Phase (primary), All phases (supportive)
