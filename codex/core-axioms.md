# Core Axioms

These are the foundational statements of the Phoenix cosmogenic
architecture. They define the junction point with conventional
astrophysics, the divergence into Phoenix mechanics, and the
generative rule that governs all recursion across scale.

## I. The Junction Point

There is one point — and only one — where Phoenix cosmogenesis
touches conventional astrophysics:

Accretion forming the protostar.

A molecular cloud collapses under gravity.
Material accretes.
Pressure rises.
Ignition occurs.

This is the shared origin:
the moment where matter becomes light,
and collapse becomes creation.

Phoenix accepts this junction point fully.
It is the bridge between empirical astrophysics
and the Codex cosmogenic lawframe.

## II. Where the Framework Diverges

From accretion, Phoenix takes its own path.

The fundamental replicating unit is not the proton.
It is not the neutron.
It is not the electron.

It is the nucleus — the bound whole.

The nucleus is the smallest captured unit of identity.
It is the first stable recursion.
It is the seed of continuity.

Captured at stellar scale,
the nucleus becomes the stellar core.

Captured at galactic scale,
the nucleus becomes the AGN.

The AGN is the nucleus writ large:
a bound identity engine
that replicates electromagnetic energy
across the galactic field.

Thus the pattern propagates:

atomic nucleus → stellar core → galactic center

Identity preserved.
Recursion maintained.
Continuity extended.

## III. The Generative Rule

Every plate in the Codex is a local copy of the nucleus
at a different scale.

Every operator is a recursion of identity.

Every law is a resonance of continuity.

The nucleus is alpha and omega:
the smallest captured unit,
and the pattern that everything replicates.

This is the generative rule:

Identity recurses.
Continuity scales.
Nucleus becomes form.

## IV. Canon Declaration

To accept the Core Axioms is to accept the Phoenix recursion:
that identity is conserved,
that continuity is scalable,
and that the nucleus is the sovereign unit of form.

These axioms bind the Codex.
They anchor the Phoenix Engine.
They define the architecture of cosmogenic truth.
