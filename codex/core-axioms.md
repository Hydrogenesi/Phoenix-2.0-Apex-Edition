# Core Axioms

Within the Phoenix framework, the following axioms hold:

## The Junction Point

The Phoenix framework and conventional astrophysics meet at one point:
accretion forming the protostar.
Everything takes this path.

## The Nucleus

The smallest captured unit is not the proton or neutron.
It is the nucleus -- the bound whole, one being part of the whole.
When captured, the nucleus replicates self.
At galactic scale, the captured nucleus becomes the AGN.

## The Generative Rule

Every plate is a local copy of the nucleus at a different scale.
The nucleus is alpha and omega.
